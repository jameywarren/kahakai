Go to the beach

A beach to sit by. Things wash up now and then. Take them or leave them. Nothing is counted, and nothing is lost when you go.

For anyone. Best with headphones.

This beach, Hanalei Bay, is free to visit here. Two more beaches, Polihale and Punaluʻu, will come with the full game, which is not out yet.

Lit in Blender and drawn in code by one person working with Claude, an AI. The sound is made by code, not recorded. No image generator was used. One texture, the wood behind the shelf, is free-licence, from Poly Haven.

What you find is kept on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you put a message in a bottle, and then only its words go, with no name on them.
