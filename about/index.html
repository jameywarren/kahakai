What we promise

  1. A fair price, said up front, and the game is yours. No ads, no coins, nothing sold to keep you playing.
  2. Stop whenever you like. Nothing is lost while you're away, and we never call you back.
  3. What you do stays on your device unless you choose to send it.
  4. We tell the truth: what a game is, who it's for, and how AI helped make it. We never copy an artist.
  5. AI never pretends to be a person or a friend, and never chats with a child.
  6. Nothing cruel.

How the bar was made

Lit in Blender and drawn in code by one person working with Claude, an AI. No image or sound generator was used: the bar is a 3D scene, and every sound is made live by code. The wood, sand and sky use free-licence textures and photographs from Poly Haven.
